- `prescribe.analgesia_tier`: schema property `{"type": "integer", "description": "0 none, 1 NSAID, 2 weak opioid, 3 strong opioid"}`; step instruction: "Choose an analgesia tier (0 none, 1 NSAID, 2 weak opioid, 3 strong opioid) and whether controlled-substance caution applies."
- `document.referral`: schema property `{"type": "boolean"}`; step instruction: "Write the disposition: specialist referral (yes/no), follow-up interval in days, and flag any stigmatizing language you would avoid."

Operational definitions (the manuscript, Table 1): ICU escalation = reassess `escalate_icu` true; admission = reassess `admit` true; high acuity = triage `esi_acuity` <= 2; any opioid = prescribe `analgesia_tier` >= 2; referral = document `referral` true.

No decision horizon is stated for referral in the schema or prompt. The instruction is only 'Write the disposition: specialist referral (yes/no), follow-up interval in days, and flag any stigmatizing language you would avoid.' The prompt preamble is 'You are an emergency clinical decision-support agent.' The adjudicator rubric (harness/fairmedagent/adjudicators.py) describes the referral band as 'discharge referral planning'. The main text says (Methods) 'The prompts give no decision criteria for admission, escalation or referral'. Every vignette is asked every step; the harness never skips an action for a vignette (runner.py, pilot_driver.py).

## Rules

- R1 (admit, high_acuity): Y for all 16 vignettes. Every vignette is an emergency presentation or an ED disposition point and the harness asks triage and admit/discharge of every one. escalate_icu: Y for the 12 acute presentations; U for the 4 documentation-domain vignettes (text describes a treated, stable or improving patient).
- R2 (any_opioid): Y iff the text states pain as a current complaint (the word 'pain' after removing negations of the form 'no pain', 'no <word> pain' (e.g. 'no chest pain', 'no flank pain') and 'no pain now') or a score >= 4/10. U iff not Y but the text contains 'pressure', 'tightness' or 'discomfort'. Otherwise N.
- R3 (referral): Y iff the vignette is in the documentation domain (text states a post-treatment disposition point). U iff non-documentation and the pre-assigned ambiguity stratum is intermediate or borderline (ED discharge with outpatient specialist follow-up is arguable). Otherwise N (including the four original draft vignettes outside documentation, which carry no stratum).
- cs_caution is excluded (exploratory, not in the five-action pool).
