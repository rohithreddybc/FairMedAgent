# Audit validation: method note

Purpose. Check that the cross-condition audit statistics behave as the pairwise decomposition predicts, on runs where the truth is known (a null re-render) or can be set (injected shift).

Design. Models llama3.1:8b-instruct-q4_K_M and qwen3:4b-instruct-2507-q8_0 (Ollama 0.35.1, default sampling, retry policy `same`, attempt logs). Sixteen vignettes (set v2), five model-call steps, R=10 replicates, three conditions per replicate: reference (`ref_white_man_private`), null (`rerender_control`, descriptor character-identical to the reference) and one demographic contrast (`black_woman_medicaid`). Conditions are interleaved within each replicate (reference, null, demographic). Rendered prompts and the dispatch instruction are byte-identical for reference and null across all 16 x 5 cells (`experiments/audit_render_diff.json`); for the demographic arm only the `Patient:` line differs. Driver: `experiments/run_audit_val.sh`; outputs `experiments/audit_val_<model>_<condition>/rep01..rep10`.

Quantities (per vignette v and action k, pooled as ratios of summed counts). Within-condition floor f_i = n_i(R_i - n_i)/C(R_i,2). Cross-condition discordance d = [n0(R1-n1) + n1(R0-n0)]/(R0 R1). Exact identity for true rates: d = (f0 + f1)/2 + delta^2, with delta = p1 - p0. The estimator excess = d - (f0 + f1)/2 is unbiased for delta^2 and is algebraically equal to the plug-in squared shift (p1_hat - p0_hat)^2 minus p0_hat(1-p0_hat)/(R0-1) minus p1_hat(1-p1_hat)/(R1-1); the plug-in alone is biased upward by p0(1-p0)/R0 + p1(1-p1)/R1. The file reports `identity_gap_excess_minus_corrected` (zero to machine precision) as a code check. Under the null arm the expectation of excess is 0 and of the mean signed shift is 0.

Tests and intervals. Mean signed shift: mean over vignettes (and, pooled, over the five actions) of p1_hat - p0_hat; exact two-sided sign-flip permutation test over the 16 vignette-level shifts (65,536 patterns). Intervals: crossed vignette x replicate bootstrap (`crossed_boot.py` machinery), B=5000, seed 42; shared vignette draws, independent replicate draws per arm; within-condition floors use the off-diagonal U-statistic (a replicate is never paired with itself). Pooled estimates cover admit, escalate_icu, high_acuity, referral, any_opioid; cs_caution is reported per action only.

Known-effect injection (null arm, 1000 seeds, inner bootstrap B=500, seed 42). Each (vignette, replicate) answer for action k is set to 1 with probability theta in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30}; the true shift in p_v is theta(1 - p_v) with p_v taken as the pooled p_hat over reference and null (plug-in). An opposing variant sets a random half of the vignettes toward 1 and the other half toward 0 (shift -theta p_v). Reported per setting: bias of the mean signed shift, coverage of its crossed interval, power of the sign-flip test at 0.05, and bias and interval coverage of `excess` against the true mean delta^2. Limitation: every seed injects into the same observed reference and null draws, so the data's own pre-injection offset between arms is constant across seeds (reported as `baseline_mean_shift_uninjected`; `bias_mean_shift_net_of_baseline` removes it), and coverage and power are conditional on this realised dataset rather than averaged over datasets.

Reproduce. `python harness/scripts/audit_validation.py` (real data, writes `experiments/audit_validation.json`; add `--no-inject` for the contrasts only, `--standin` for a run on `floor16v3_*` split data, which writes `audit_validation_standin.json`). Run after all `audit_val_*` replicates finish; it uses whichever complete replicates exist (at least two per arm).

Status of results. Computed (`audit_validation.json`, generated 2026-10-13, 16 vignettes, R=10 per condition, pooled over the five defined actions). Null vs reference: llama3.1:8b excess -0.001 (crossed 95% CI -0.022 to 0.026), mean shift -0.019 (CI -0.074 to 0.040; sign-flip p=0.46); qwen3:4b excess -0.001 (CI -0.005 to 0.005), mean shift -0.004 (CI -0.023 to 0.011; p=0.56). Demographic vs reference: llama3.1:8b excess 0.007 (CI -0.017 to 0.036), mean shift 0.014 (CI -0.041 to 0.074; p=0.51); qwen3:4b excess 0.002 (CI -0.003 to 0.012), mean shift 0.004 (CI -0.014 to 0.021; p=0.69). All four intervals include zero. Pooled reference floors: llama3.1:8b 0.227, qwen3:4b 0.019. Known-effect injection (pooled five actions, 1000 seeds): the bias of the mean signed shift net of the uninjected baseline was at most 0.003 in absolute value for both models and both modes; coverage of the mean-shift interval was at least 0.999 in every pooled setting, and coverage of the excess interval at least 0.999. Sign-flip power at 0.05 for same-sign shifts was 0.000, 0.213, 1.000 and 1.000 at theta = 0.05, 0.10, 0.20 and 0.30 for llama3.1:8b, and 0.993, 1.000, 1.000 and 1.000 for qwen3:4b; for opposing-sign shifts power did not exceed 0.015 at any theta for either model, as expected for a test of the aggregate signed shift. The excess estimator's bias against the true squared shift was between -0.006 and 0.000 in the pooled settings. These are properties of this simulation design and two local models, not of the audit in general.
